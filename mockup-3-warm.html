Questions We Hear All the Time

Can the IRS really settle for less than I owe?

Yes — through a program called Offer in Compromise, the IRS can accept less than the full balance owed if you meet certain criteria. In 2023, the IRS accepted over 13,000 offers. We'll tell you honestly whether you're a good candidate.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the resolution type. A levy release can happen in days. An Offer in Compromise typically takes 6–18 months. We'll give you a realistic timeline from the start.

Will you judge me for getting into this situation?

Absolutely not. Tax problems happen to good people — business failures, medical crises, divorce, job loss, confusion about self-employment taxes. We've heard it all. We're here to solve it, not to make you feel worse.

What does it cost?

We charge flat fees based on the type of case — no hourly billing, no surprise invoices. We'll give you a firm quote after your free consultation, and we only move forward if you're comfortable with the number.
